OKX separates a simple buy flow from spot trading. The buy function uses a quoted purchase route, while spot trading uses an order book where the user selects an order type and price.

Once the order completes, check the asset in your portfolio and review the order history. Do not judge the result only by the portfolio’s current value. The value can change immediately because crypto prices move, and the final portfolio amount may reflect market movement, the quoted price, foreign-exchange conversion, spread, or payment fees.

### Buy, Convert, and spot trading are different

**Buy** lets you purchase supported crypto through an available payment method or account balance.

**Convert** swaps one eligible crypto asset for another using a quote. It is convenient, but you should still review the quote and any displayed spread.

**Spot trading** uses an order book. You can generally choose between market and limit orders, depending on the market and account. A market order executes against available liquidity, while a limit order waits for the market to reach the price you specify.

A beginner-friendly progression is:

- Use Buy for a first small purchase.
- Learn Convert after understanding quoted prices.
- Use spot trading when you understand market orders, limit orders, maker and taker fees, and order history.

## What beginners should avoid

### Futures and margin

Futures and margin allow traders to control positions with borrowed exposure or leverage. They can produce faster gains, but they can also liquidate a position when the market moves against you. A beginner who is still learning deposits and spot orders has no practical reason to start here.

### Trading bots

Bots can automate strategies, but automation does not make a bad strategy good. A bot can continue placing orders while the market changes, fees accumulate, or the user misunderstands the configuration.

### Earn products

Yield products may involve lending, staking, liquidity, or other mechanisms. Read the asset, term, redemption rules, risks, and estimated rate before using them. “Earn” does not mean guaranteed interest.

### Unfamiliar tokens

Check the asset name, ticker, network, contract address, liquidity, and withdrawal support. Fake tokens can use the same symbol as a legitimate asset. On-chain transfers are often irreversible.

### Sending everything to an exchange

An exchange can be convenient for active trading, but it is not the only place to store assets. For longer-term holdings, learn the difference between exchange custody and self-custody. A self-custody wallet gives you control of the keys, but it also makes you responsible for seed-phrase security and transaction mistakes.
